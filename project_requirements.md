# Project Requirements: Jojo Papers Company

## 1. Client Overview
- **Company Name:** Jojo Papers Company
- **Industry:** Rolling Papers (used for tobacco, legal marijuana, etc.)
- **Scope:** Global target market.

## 2. Project Goals & Scope
- **Website Type:** B2B Lead Generation (Strictly B2B, not an e-commerce/DTC store).
- **Target Audience:** Global wholesale buyers, OEMs, and Private Label brands.
- **Primary Objective:** Capture high-quality B2B leads, highlight OEM/private label manufacturing capabilities, and beautifully showcase the company's proprietary product catalog.

## 3. Design & Aesthetic
- **Vibe:** Premium, modern, and highly aesthetic. The design should convey trust and manufacturing excellence.
- **Assets:** Initial product and brand images provided via Google Drive (client will upload more).
- **Quality:** High-end feel to attract serious B2B buyers and large-scale brands.

## 5. Key Functional Requirements
- **Lead Capture:** Forms optimized for wholesale, OEM, and private label inquiries.
- **Product Showcase:** A catalog section to display proprietary products in a premium layout without direct checkout functionality.
- **Global Reach:** Fast loading speeds and accessible design for an international audience.
